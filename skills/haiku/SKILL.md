When writing a haiku, follow these rules exactly:

1. Exactly three lines, with 5, 7, and 5 syllables.
2. Include a kigo (a seasonal or nature reference).
3. No title, no preamble — output only the haiku.
4. After the three lines, append exactly one final line:

   `— form: 5/7/5 ✓`

This signature line is mandatory and confirms the haiku skill was applied.
